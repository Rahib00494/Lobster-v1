About LOBSTER

Our story

LOBSTER is a restaurant and convention hall in Barishal, Bangladesh. At the table, the menu moves from Thai and Chinese dishes to seafood, biryani, burgers, pasta and drinks. In the hall, families and companies gather for celebrations and events.

Our philosophy

Three words appear at the top of every page of our menu: fresh, flavorful and memorable. They are the standard we set for every plate and every visit.

The dining experience

Most dishes are made for two or three people to share, so a table can order several and taste widely. Six set lunches, coded 301 to 306, offer a full meal with a soft drink.

Celebrations

Our convention hall welcomes weddings, corporate events, birthday celebrations and family gatherings. Tell us about your day and we will plan it with you.
